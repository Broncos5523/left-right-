How this diagnostic is used

A replay advances through recorded plays and reveals which player IDs ESPN actually supplies. It tests parsing and retention opportunities, not the provider's live latency, historical game-day inactives, or complete 11/22 participation. A postgame box score would reveal future statistics, so this page deliberately does not use final box-score totals as if they were live.

Historical ESPN site API is unofficial and may stop working. The separate football-personnel QA document records outstanding integration issues.
